# Contributing to the community list

A row in this list says, in public, that something is not what it claims to be. Getting that wrong hurts a
real creator, so the bar is deliberately high.

## To report an item as not the creator's

Open a pull request adding a row to `## Not mine` with:

- `platform` and `id` exactly as they appear on the platform (see `docs/list-format.md`).
- `title` as shown on the item page.
- `first seen` as an ISO date.
- `note`: what makes this a hijack, and where it was reported. Link your evidence.
- `source`: the creator's own list URL if this came from one.

A row is merged when **either**:

1. the creator confirms it, in public or to a maintainer, and the PR links to that; **or**
2. two curators who are not the submitter sign off in the PR thread, each stating what they checked.

## What is not evidence

These are worth mentioning in a note, and are never sufficient on their own:

- A distributor placeholder label such as `8412 Records DK`. Most legitimate independent releases look like this.
- "Independently published" with no reviews. That describes a large share of honest self-publishing.
- A title resembling another title. Summaries, workbooks and companions can be legitimate, and genres reuse phrases.
- An opinion that the work sounds or reads like it was AI-generated.

## To add baseline rows

`## Likely accurate` rows are generated by the `baseline-catalog` job from public catalog data and are
date-derived only: the item was released before the `Baseline-before` cutoff. Do not hand-add rows to claim a
work is genuine for any other reason.

## Removal

Any creator can have rows about them removed by asking, with no justification required. Open an issue or
contact a maintainer. If a creator publishes their own verified list, it takes precedence and our rows for
their profile are deleted rather than merged.
